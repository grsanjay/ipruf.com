How it works

  1. Capture — photo, annotation, voice note, optional crew selfie
  2. Stamp — date/time, GPS and a Google-Maps QR on every exported photo, using the phone's time and location at capture. Original untouched. Imported photos are labeled “Imported” with the import time and place.
  3. AI — turns your photos and voice notes into a clean customer report. You approve before sending.
  4. Send — branded PDF with the stamped photos attached.

  • Free — 3 jobs with full export. After that: view-only in the app, SAMPLE-watermarked previews.
  • Basic $9.99/mo — unlimited jobs, clean PDF export, stamped photos, annotation, voice notes, checklists, customer acceptance/sign-off, scheduling and period reports.

Built for owner-operators and small crews where the person capturing is the person sending the report. No dispatcher dashboard, no shared office workspace — by design, your jobs stay in your own iCloud.

What AI Pro adds

  • Your words, your format — custom AI report instructions. Tell the AI once how you like your reports written — bulleted checklist, materials used at the end — and every draft follows it.
  • Your brand only — AI Pro removes the “Made with iPruf” footer. The report your customer gets carries your company name, front to back.
  • Import up to 20 photos at once — in every plan: camera-roll photos join the job in one pick, stamped “Imported” with the import time and place. No back-dating, ever.

AI Pro uses your own OpenAI key — a one-time, ~3-minute setup in the app. The key stays in your phone’s Keychain, iPruf never sees it, and OpenAI bills you directly for what you use.
