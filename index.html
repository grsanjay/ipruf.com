How it works

  1. Capture — photo, annotation, voice note, optional crew selfie
  2. Stamp — date/time, GPS and a Google-Maps QR on every exported photo, using the phone's time and location at capture. Original untouched. Imported photos are labeled “Imported” with the import time and place.
  3. AI — turns your photos and voice notes into a clean customer report. You approve before sending.
  4. Send — branded PDF with the stamped photos attached.

Questions, answered

How does iPruf work?

You capture photos and voice notes on the job. iPruf stamps every exported photo with the date, time, GPS location and a Google-Maps QR code — the original photo is never modified. With AI Pro, your notes become a clean customer report that you approve and send as a PDF. Captured. Stamped. Sent.

Which trades does it cover?

Ten templates: Contractors, Service Techs, Roofers, Cleaners, Property, Site Teams, Delivery, Process Servers and Punch Lists — plus a General template for any other field work. Pick yours when you start, switch any time in Settings. Every job keeps the template it started with.

Do I need an iCloud account?

Yes. Your jobs live in your own iCloud account — there is no iPruf server and we never see your photos. iCloud is also what makes the free tier fair: 3 free jobs per Apple ID, and they don’t reset if you delete and reinstall the app.

How does the free tier work?

Your first 3 jobs are full-featured, with clean PDF export. After that you can keep capturing and viewing in the app; exporting waits until you subscribe. And when you subscribe, your free count resets — if you later cancel, you get a fresh 3.

Basic or AI Pro — what’s the difference?

Basic $9.99/mo: unlimited jobs, stamped photos, annotation, voice notes, checklists, customer sign-off, scheduling and period reports, clean PDFs with a small “Made with iPruf” footer. AI Pro $19.99/mo adds AI-drafted reports from your photos and voice notes, your own custom AI instructions, AI period summaries — and reports that carry only your brand, no iPruf footer. Both are Founding Pricing, locked for early users while we grow.

Why do I need my own OpenAI key for the AI?

So your data and your costs stay yours. You add your key once (about 3 minutes — step-by-step on the Support page). It stays in your phone’s Keychain, iPruf never sees it, and OpenAI bills you directly, only for what you use. Nothing is sent to AI without your consent.

Does iPruf certify or verify my work?

No — by design. iPruf records what was captured, when and where. It never issues pass/fail judgments, certifications or valuations. Your customer gets a clear, time-stamped record — not a verdict.

Can my customer sign off on a report?

Yes. They can review and sign on your phone, or confirm later with the approval code that travels with the report. The record reads “Customer acknowledged receipt of this report” — a receipt record that settles most “I never got it” disputes.

What languages does it speak?

English, Spanish and Portuguese — and the three are independent: app screens, voice-note transcription and the report language are each set separately. Your crew can work in Spanish and send the customer an English report.

What happens if I cancel?

Nothing is deleted and nothing is held hostage — your jobs stay in your iCloud. Resubscribe any time and you pick up right where you left off.
